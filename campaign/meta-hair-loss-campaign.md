# Meta campaign: Not Bald. Not Too Early.

## Strategic idea

The campaign is for the stage before an obvious gap: the audience is noticing change, shedding, stress-related thinning, or reduced density, but does not identify as “bald” and is not looking for a transplant. The emotional tension is not vanity; it is uncertainty about whether the change is real and whether it is too early to act.

**Big idea:** Not Bald. Not Too Early.

**Action line:** Check the change before the gap.

**Offer:** A private, no-cost, 60-second photo analysis that helps someone understand the visible pattern and choose an appropriate next step. It is an assessment aid, not a diagnosis.

## Campaign structure

### Campaign 1 — UK prospecting

- Objective: Leads, website conversion location.
- Primary conversion: completed hair analysis. Secondary: qualified consultation request.
- Geography: United Kingdom, with a separate reporting split for London and Glasgow clinic catchments.
- Age: 25–55. Do not target teens.
- Audience: broad/Advantage+ audience with only geography and age as hard controls. Meta does not offer sensitive health-condition audience options, so let the creative qualify the viewer.
- Placements: Advantage+ placements; supply a dedicated 9:16 asset for Reels and Stories and a 4:5 crop for Feed.
- Initial budget split: 70% broad prospecting, 20% warm retargeting, 10% creative testing reserve.
- Optimization: begin on completed analysis if volume supports stable delivery; otherwise optimize to landing-page view for a short learning phase, then graduate to completed analysis.

### Campaign 2 — warm retargeting

- Audience A: watched at least 50% of a campaign video in the last 14 days.
- Audience B: opened or started the analysis but did not complete in the last 14 days.
- Audience C: completed the analysis but did not book in the last 30 days.
- Exclude recent booked consultations and existing patients where appropriate.
- Sequence: honesty → clinical proof → consultation reassurance.

## Creative 01 — cold master (built)

- Format: 1080×1920, 30 fps, 9.5 seconds.
- Hook: “NOT BALD.”
- Reframe: “NOT TOO EARLY.”
- Proof: real Harley Street Medics doctor and scalp-treatment footage.
- Insight: “When the change is subtle, that may be the point.”
- Evidence beat: authentic patient imagery, labelled with outcome and assessment disclaimers.
- CTA: “Check the change before the gap. Start my hair analysis.”
- Voiceover: “Not bald. Not too early. If the change is subtle, that may be the point. Check your pattern in sixty seconds.”

Primary text:

> Early-stage hair changes can be difficult to read. A private photo analysis can make the visible pattern easier to understand—and help identify a sensible next step. Free, no obligation, around 60 seconds.

Headline: **Free 60-second hair analysis**

Description: **Doctor-led options in London & Glasgow**

## Retargeting creative sequence

### Creative 02 — “We’ll tell you if it’s too early”

- 8 seconds.
- Doctor to camera, then treatment room.
- Overlay: “Sometimes the right treatment is no treatment yet.”
- CTA: “Get a clearer next step.”
- Purpose: neutralize scepticism and demonstrate clinical honesty.

### Creative 03 — “This is what non-surgical looks like”

- 9 seconds.
- Tight, rhythmic cuts of the real VirtueRF/exosome procedure.
- Overlay: “No transplant. No theatre. A clinician-led plan.”
- CTA: “See whether treatment is worth discussing.”
- Purpose: make the process concrete for warm viewers.

### Creative 04 — “One photo, then a human”

- 8 seconds.
- Analysis UI → doctor portrait → clinic room.
- Overlay: “Start privately. Continue with a clinician only if it makes sense.”
- CTA: “Complete the analysis.”
- Purpose: recover analysis starters who did not finish.

## Policy and clinical guardrails

- Do not say or imply “you are losing your hair,” “your bald spot,” or otherwise assert a viewer’s health or appearance attribute.
- Avoid cure, guarantee, permanent-result, and universal-response claims.
- Avoid shaming, panic language, and “act now before it is too late.”
- Keep “individual outcomes vary” on transformation imagery.
- Do not represent the analysis as a medical diagnosis.
- Confirm patient consent covers paid social use before publishing any result or procedure footage.
- Meta reviews the creative, copy, targeting, and destination together, so the landing page must use the same neutral framing.

## Test matrix

Run one variable at a time:

1. Hook: “Not Bald” vs “The change can be subtle.”
2. Proof lead: doctor face vs procedure macro.
3. CTA: “Start my analysis” vs “Check the pattern.”
4. Cold master with authentic patient imagery vs the lower-risk clinic-only proof cut.

Judge first on 3-second hold and 50% view rate, then landing-page-view rate, completed-analysis rate, qualified-booking rate, and cost per qualified booking. Do not scale a high-CTR creative if qualification falls.
